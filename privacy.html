Privacy policy

Your media stays on your device during conversion.

Video To Audio & Mp3 Cutter processes selected audio and video files locally. Media permissions allow you to choose and manage files. Contact and system-settings permissions are requested only for ringtone features that need them.

When configured for release, advertising services (Google AdMob and Unity), Firebase Analytics/Crashlytics and Google Play Billing may process device identifiers, usage events, diagnostics and purchase information under their own policies. Media content is not uploaded for conversion.

When you use Settings → Send feedback, your subject, message and optional contact email are sent over HTTPS to FightTech at fighttech.vn, together with device and app details used to diagnose the issue. A screenshot is sent only if you attach one. You can leave the email blank and use the app without sending feedback. We use your email only to reply to your support request, not for advertising. Feedback is retained to handle your request; it is not processed only in memory. To request deletion of your feedback, email fighttech.vn@gmail.com. Media files are not uploaded for conversion.

Favorites and app preferences are stored on your device. You can remove local app data through Android settings. Your exported media remains in the media library until you delete it. Contact FightTech for privacy questions.
