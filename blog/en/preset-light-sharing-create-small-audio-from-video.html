Preset Light sharing: create small audio from video

FightTechSeptember 20, 20262 min read

When the recipient only needs to hear a short piece of information, you can prioritize volume over musical space. Light Share profile sets MP3 64 kbps mono and normalized. This configuration requires trial listening as not all content is suitable for low compression.

What you will do

Use the Light Share profile with MP3 64 kbps mono in Video MP4 to MP3 Converter and check the speech before sending.

Steps

  1. Select the Light sharing profile in the Sound Studio screen.
  2. Select the video and keep the part containing the content to send.
  3. Check the formatting and options, then export a copy.
  4. Listen to difficult words and check the actual size before opening the share sheet.

Speech and thick mixes have different needs

A single note may still be intelligible while a multi-instrument track loses noticeable detail. If metallic sounds or consonants are difficult to hear, increase the quality or shorten the paragraph instead of squeezing the entire content into a file that is too small. Presets help get started quickly, not replacing testing.

Practical tip

Leave some space below the recipient's file limit instead of aiming for the maximum number.
